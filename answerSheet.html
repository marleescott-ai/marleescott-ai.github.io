Answers For The Multi Choice Simple Questions

    1. Cell - Basic Unit Of Life

    2. Chromosome - A thread-like structure that contains DNA wrapped around a protein(histone). Found in the nucleus

    3. DNA - A helix-shaped, double-stranded, polynucleotide molecule which has H-bonds between adjacent bases. This molecule encodes genetic information

    4. Mutation - A random change in the base sequence (nucleotide) in DNA that can create a new allele. It can either be continuous or discontinuous

    5. Nucleus -The organelle that contains DNA

    6. Meiosis - Cell division that occurs in the gonads (reproductive organs) to create gametes/ sex cells

    7. Mitosis - Cell division in somatic cells for growth and repair

    8. Proteins - A molecule made up of many amino acids joined by peptide bonds. They have a variety of functions in the body

    9. Mutagen - Any agent that increases the chances of mutations occurring

    10. Gene - A short segment of DNA that codes for a protein for a trait

Answers For The Hard Fill-In The Blanks

    11. Semi-Conservative - The DNA molecule has one old strand and one new strand after DNA replication is complete

    12. Indepedant segreation - This refers to the random alignment of homologous chromosome and how they separate randomly

    13. Crossing over - A section of non-sister chromatids overlap at the chiasmaand switch with the other in the homologous pair

    14. Founders effect - When a small group of individuals move away from the original population, and becomes isolated. This group carries only a small proportion of the original gene pool, reducing genetic variation

    15. Bottleneck effect - When a large population is suddenly reduced in size by a random event, such as a natural disaster or human activity. This results in the loss of alleles and a reduced genetic variation in the surviving population
